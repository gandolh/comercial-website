# GDPR Document Kits

Romania has active GDPR enforcement (ANSPDCP), mandatory documents under Law 190/2018, and a clear gap in affordable sector-specific template packs. Existing competitors charge 2,900–7,500 RON for comprehensive kits; the market for lighter, cheaper, niche-targeted packs is unserved.

## Mandatory Documents (What Every Romanian Business Needs)

**Universally required:**
- **Politică de confidențialitate** — privacy policy, publicly accessible on website
- **Politică de cookies** — required for any analytics, pixels, or tracking
- **Registrul activităților de prelucrare (RoPA)** — inventory of processing activities (Art. 30; ANSPDCP expects it from all companies, not just 250+ employees)
- **Contracte de prelucrare a datelor (DPA)** — required with every third-party processor: hosting, accountants, payroll, email/CRM tools
- **Notificări de informare angajați** — employee notice at time of hire (Art. 13)
- **Acorduri de confidențialitate angajați** — employee confidentiality agreement
- **Proceduri de notificare incidente** — breach notification procedure; 72h ANSPDCP reporting window

**Situation-specific:**
- **DPIA** — for high-risk processing (CCTV, profiling, large-scale health data)
- **Proceduri drepturi persoane vizate** — handle access/deletion/portability requests within 30 days
- **Consimțământ prelucrare CNP** — Law 190/2018 special requirement for national ID number
- **Proceduri supraveghere angajați** — employee monitoring requires prior union notification + 30-day storage max
- **Numire DPO** — mandatory for public authorities, large-scale monitoring, or special category data

**For online stores specifically (GEO 141/2021):**
- Termeni și condiții, politică de retur, politică de livrare
- Cookie banner with equal accept/reject buttons (Law 506/2004 amended 2026)
- Opt-in consent records for email marketing

## What ANSPDCP Checks During Audits

Top enforcement priorities (2024–2025 patterns):
1. Missing DPAs with third-party processors — "most frequently sanctioned non-conformity"
2. Processing without valid legal basis
3. Missing information disclosures at point of collection
4. Inadequate technical security measures (Art. 32)
5. No breach notification procedure
6. Cookie dark patterns — pre-checked boxes, hidden refusal buttons
7. CCTV/GPS monitoring without proper procedures
8. Failure to respond to rights requests within 30 days

## Cookie Consent Law (2026 Amendment to Law 506/2004)

- Reject button must be at first level, equally visible as accept
- No dark patterns: no pre-checked boxes, no confusing language
- Withdrawal as easy as granting consent
- Penalties: 5,000–100,000 RON standard; up to 2% annual turnover for large companies
- Compliance window: 120 days from entry into force

## Market Gaps (What to Sell)

The large players cover mid-market companies. Underserved segments:

**By sector (sector-specific kits, ~150–500 RON each):**
- PFA / freelancer pack — almost no coverage; existing kits target SRL
- Restaurant / cafe / food delivery
- Beauty salon, barbershop, nail studio (client data + CCTV + loyalty)
- Medical / dental clinic (patient data + Law 46/2003)
- School / daycare / tutoring (children's data = special category)
- Real estate agency (prospect data retained long-term)
- Fitness / gym (membership + health metrics)

**By document type (standalone templates, ~50–150 RON):**
- Combined GDPR + eCommerce starter (privacy + terms + returns + cookie — 6–8 docs, ~€50–100 entry point)
- App / mobile privacy policy with App Store / Play Store language
- Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) templates for B2B marketing
- DPIA templates for specific use cases (loyalty programs, employee monitoring software)
- HR onboarding pack (employment addendum + notice + confidentiality + monitoring policy as one set)

## Pricing Strategy

- Sector starter packs: 150–400 RON (vs 2,900 RON competitors = 7–13x cheaper)
- Full freelancer/PFA kit: 300 RON
- eCommerce starter (6 docs): 200 RON
- Premium sector pack (15+ docs): 400–600 RON
