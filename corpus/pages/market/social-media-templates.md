# Social Media Templates for Romanian SMBs

42.3% of Romanian e-commerce stores have zero content budget despite heavy ad spend (Zelist.ro 2024). The gap between self-serve template packs (~€15–40) and agency management (~€450/month) is largely unoccupied by Romanian-language, Romanian-calendar-aware template sellers.

|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | 12.9M | 68.3% | Stable |

**Key takeaways:**
- Facebook is the largest by volume but stagnant; still dominant 45–54 age group
- TikTok + Instagram are the growth engines; skew young but broadening
- LinkedIn +12.8% signals rising B2B demand — HR, real estate, B2B services templates needed
- Pinterest +28.7% matters for visual niches: interiors, food, crafts, fashion

## Content Formats That Perform Best

- **Short video / Reels** — highest reach and discovery on Instagram; TikTok is native format
- **TikTok live shopping** — 3–5× higher conversion than standard posts
- **Carousels** — educational content, before/after, product features
- **Stories** — daily engagement, polls, flash offers
- **Static posts** — announcements, branding consistency, promotions

## Romanian Marketing Calendar (Full)

Template packs should include designs for all major dates. Competitors ignore most of these.

**January**
- Jan 1–2: Anul Nou (New Year)
- Jan 6: Boboteaza — name day marketing opportunity
- Jan 7: Sfântul Ioan — large name day
- Jan 24: Ziua Unirii Principatelor (national history)

**February**
- Feb 14: Valentine's Day — internationally adopted, moderate local uptake
- Feb 24: **Dragobete** — Romanian Valentine's Day (Dacian origin); strong differentiator vs international brands; use for gift shops, restaurants, florists

**March**
- Mar 1: **Mărțișor** — biggest spring commercial event; red-and-white motifs; huge for gift, beauty, food, jewelry
- Mar 8: Ziua Internațională a Femeii — major commercial event; flowers, gifts, beauty

**April**
- **Florii** (Orthodox Palm Sunday, moveable) — name day: Florina/Florin/Flora
- **Vinerea Mare** (Good Friday)
- **Paștele Ortodox** (Orthodox Easter, moveable) — **single largest cultural-commercial event**; 2026 date April 12; weeks of content opportunity: traditional food, ouă încondeiate, family themes. Note: often different date from Catholic Easter — use correct Orthodox date

**May**
- May 1: Ziua Muncii — outdoor/BBQ content
- May 3: Ziua Mamei (Mother's Day)
- May 10: Ziua Tatălui (Father's Day)
- Rusaliile (Pentecost, moveable — May 31, 2026)

**June**
- Jun 1: **Ziua Copilului** — massive for toy, food, education brands
- Jun 24: **Ziua Universală a Iei** — International Day of the Romanian Blouse; viral each year on Instagram; strong for fashion, artisan, cultural brands

**July–August**
- Aug 15: Adormirea Maicii Domnului — major Orthodox celebration; relevant for gifts, monasteries, rural tourism
- Aug 31: Ziua Limbii Române — growing relevance for education brands

**September**
- Back to School (~Sep 8–15)

**October**
- Oct 31: Halloween — adopted by younger urban demographics, cafes, costume shops

**November**
- **Black Friday** — Romania's biggest e-commerce event; starts early November; eMAG defines the national moment
- Nov 30: Sfântul Andrei — national holiday; patron saint of Romania

**December**
- Dec 1: **Ziua Națională a României** — national pride, tricolor content
- Dec 6: Moș Nicolae — children's gifts
- Dec 25–26: Crăciun (Christmas)
- Dec 31: Revelion (New Year's Eve)

**Year-round:**
- **Onomastice (name days)** — deeply culturally significant; commercial opportunity for personalized content (Maria Aug 15, Ion Jan 7, Andrei Nov 30, Florin/Florina on Florii)
- **Postul Paștelui / Postul Crăciunului** (Lent) — relevant for food brands offering fasting menus

## Most Active Romanian Industries

Template packs should be industry-specific:

1. **Restaurante și food** — daily posting, stories of dishes, seasonal specials, delivery promos
2. **Frumusețe și estetică** — salons, barbershops, spa, nail studios; before/after, appointment promos
3. **Imobiliare** — property tours, market updates; LinkedIn growing strongly for this vertical
4. **Stomatologie și clinici medicale** — educational content, appointment calls to action; strict GDPR constraints
5. **Retail modă și accesorii** — lookbooks, product showcases, seasonal campaigns
6. **Fitness și wellness** — challenge content, transformations, class schedules
7. **Construcții și amenajări interioare** — strong on Pinterest and Instagram
8. **E-commerce generalist** — Black Friday, Easter, Christmas heavy-hitters

## Romanian Design Conventions

- **Colors:** Warm, direct palettes work best. Red-and-white (Mărțișor) has deep cultural resonance. Traditional geometric patterns (from ie, covor) are in a fashion revival. Blue = professional/B2B trust.
- **Typography:** Inter, Roboto, Montserrat for modern brands. Sans-serif preferred for digital. Handwritten / folk accents signal authenticity for food and artisan brands.
- **Style:** Denser, warmer than Western European minimalism. Romanian SMBs underperform with English-only copy in domestic campaigns — Romanian-language CTAs ("Comandă acum", "Rezervă loc", "Află mai mult") expected.

## Pricing Model

- Single holiday pack (10–15 designs, 1 occasion): 30–60 RON (~€6–12)
- Industry pack (30 designs, multiple formats for one sector): 80–150 RON (~€16–30)
- Full annual bundle (all holidays + industry templates): 250–400 RON (~€50–80)
